Don't Melt Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Don't Melt ("the game") uses, and why. The game is made by Prisoner, an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email pr1s0ner@msn.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we don't collect or receive your personal information.
  • Your progress is saved only on your phone.
  • The game shows optional ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some information about your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Information saved on your device

The game saves the following on your phone so you can carry on where you left off:

  • Your ice cubes and the wardrobe items you have bought and are wearing.
  • Your leaderboard entries: the name you type in, your score, the difficulty, how far you got and how many revives you used.
  • Your sound, music and vibration settings.

This information never leaves your phone. We cannot see it, and it is not shared with anyone. The leaderboard is local to your device; it is not an online leaderboard. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and is paid for by rewarded ads. You only see an ad when you choose to watch one, for example to get extra snowballs, revive after melting or double your ice cubes. The game has no banner ads and no ads that appear without you asking.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To serve ads, limit how often you see them, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game collects and sends to Google:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers.
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location (such as your country or city).
  • Information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language.
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps.
  • Crash and performance information about the ads.

This information is sent over an encrypted connection. Google handles it under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not receive this information ourselves, apart from summary reports from AdMob (for example, how many ads were shown in each country) that don't identify anyone.

Your choices about ads

  • UK, European Economic Area and Switzerland: the first time you open the game, a consent form from Google asks whether you agree to personalised ads. If you don't agree, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on things like your general location and the game rather than your interests. We rely on your consent for personalised advertising.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for advertising.
  • Changing your mind: open Settings in the game and tap Privacy Options. This button appears in the regions where these choices apply.
  • On any Android phone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually under Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads).
  • You never have to watch an ad to play the game.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for teenagers. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you have concerns, please contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it is used. Because we don't hold any personal information about you, information held by Google for advertising is best managed through the options above and through Google, at myadcenter.google.com. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Permissions

The game uses internet access (to load ads) and vibration (for feedback during play, which you can turn off in Settings). It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
