Don't Melt

Run. Hide. Don't melt.

Snowy the snowman is lost in a hedge maze, and the leprechauns want him gone. Find the key, reach the door and keep going before the fires catch up.

Coming soon to Google Play

A new maze every time

Every maze is generated fresh, with loops instead of dead ends. Clear 5, 10 or 25 in a row, or try Insane, which never ends.

Fight back with snow

Pelt red, blue and sneaky ninja leprechauns with snowballs, and put out their fires before they spread through the hedges.

Mystery presents

Open presents for Triple Shot, Starburst, Fireworks, an Ice Beam, an Ice Shield, a Blizzard Bomb and more.

Dress up Snowy

Earn ice cubes every run and spend them on a range of hats, coats, scarves, capes and extras. Try anything on before you buy.

Free to play. Ads are always optional. You only see one when you choose to, for a reward.
